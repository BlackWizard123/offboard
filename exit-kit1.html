JAN – FEB 2027

Money to track

Full & Final Settlement (FFS) takes 30–45 working days after LWD — roughly late January to mid‑February 2027. A few things are worth confirming rather than assuming.

Ask HR to confirm your December salary status verify

The letter's wording on the "before 15th" hold rule is genuinely ambiguous for a mid‑month LWD like yours. Don't guess — ask your RM or HR directly whether December pay is held and released with your FFS.

Check your Variable Pay eligibility verify

Advance and Reconciled Variable Pay for the final month only pay out if LWD falls on the last calendar day of the month. Since 16 Dec isn't the last day of December, confirm whether you lose this payout entirely.

Check your gratuity eligibility

Paid only with 4 years 10 months or more of continuous service. Work out your own tenure against this before expecting it in your FFS.

Watch your personal inbox for FFS documents

Positive FFS → documents emailed to your personal address. Negative FFS → the TechM FFS recovery team emails you recovery details and the bank account to pay into. Your leave balance gets encashed as part of this too.
